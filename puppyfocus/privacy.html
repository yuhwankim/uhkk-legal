Puppy Focus — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-11-03 · v1.3

Puppy Focus ("the app", Korean title 멍 포커스) is a focus timer provided by individual developer uhkk x studio (Yuhwan Kim). The app has no account or login, no ads or analytics SDKs, and sends no data to any developer server.

1. Information processed

Focus history (start/end times, duration, completion status and counts, gifts), dog/theme/name choices and sound/notification settings are stored only on your device to keep your progress. They are never sent to the developer. If Android auto-backup is on, they may be included in your own Google account device backup (for restoring on a new phone; you can turn this off in system settings). The app does not collect your name, email, phone number, location, photos, contacts or advertising ID.

2. Permissions

Notifications — used only to tell you when a focus or rest session ends. You can deny or change it at any time in system settings.

3. Sharing

None. No data is shared with third parties.

4. Deleting data

Uninstall the app or clear its storage (Settings → Apps → Puppy Focus → Storage → Clear data). There is no server-side data to delete.

5. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and collects no personal information from anyone.
